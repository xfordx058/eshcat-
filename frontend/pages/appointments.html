Schedule a visit with a municipal office. No account required.

How it works

  1. Request
    Pick an office, date, and time.
  2. Confirmation
    You'll get a CAT-APT reference number.
  3. Visit
    Bring your reference number to the municipal hall.

Office hours are Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM. This is a demo — no real appointments are scheduled.
