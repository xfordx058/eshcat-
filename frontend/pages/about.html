About eSHCAT

Electronic Services Hub for Catarman — one municipality, connected services, easier access.

What is eSHCAT?

Digital services for the people of Catarman

eSHCAT is a hackathon prototype that showcases how the Municipality of Catarman, Northern Samar could bring its everyday services online — from civil registry certificates and business permits to appointments and community reporting. Built entirely with open web technologies, it works on any device and stays usable even without a stable connection.

Important Notice

eSHCAT is a hackathon prototype. No real government records are used — all applications, appointments, and reports are demo data. Verify official requirements and fees directly with the municipal office.

Accessible

Works on phone, tablet, and desktop. Designed for everyone in the community.

Transparent

Real-time status updates let applicants see exactly where their request stands.

Efficient

Fewer trips to the municipal hall, faster preparation, and simpler follow-ups.

Modern

Up-to-date web standards: secure, offline-capable, and PWA-ready.

Built around the community

The services in this hub mirror the real offerings of the Municipal Government of Catarman — from the Local Civil Registry, Business Permits and Licensing, the Treasurer's Office, the Municipal Assessor, the Office of the Building Official, and more.

Made with care

Crafted with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on a Flask backend with SQLite for data. eSHCAT demonstrates that meaningful digital government can start small and simple.
